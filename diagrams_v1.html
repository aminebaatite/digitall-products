How compound numbers are built

English says the tens first and the units second. Darija reverses this: you say the unit, then the connector o (and), then the ten.

ENGLISH — LEFT TO RIGHT
twenty
five
reads 20 → 5
DARIJA — RIGHT TO LEFT
Hamsa
five (unit)
o
and
3ashreen
twenty (ten)
said unit first, then the ten
25
Hamsa o 3ashreen
خمسة و عشرين
41
Wahed o rbe3in
واحد و ربعين
22
Tnayn o 3ashreen
note: Jouj becomes Tnayn

The connector o never changes. Only the unit and the ten swap position relative to English.
