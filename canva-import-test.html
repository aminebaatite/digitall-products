MODULE 2 · GREETINGS & SOCIAL RHYTHM

Hellos, Goodbyes & Well-Wishes

Greetings in Morocco are rarely rushed. Taking a moment to greet someone properly is a sign of respect, and it is one of the first things that marks you out as someone making a genuine effort.
