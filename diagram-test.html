Where these sounds are made

Each of these five sounds is produced at a different point between the throat and the lips. The deeper the point, the further back in the throat you shape the sound.

3
gh
kh

3'Ayn — deep in the throat, constricted. عشرة
ghGhayn — gargled, like a French r. غادي
khKha — raspy, as in Scottish "loch". خويا
qQaf — a k made further back. قهوة
hHa — light and breathy, from the chest. هادي

Positions are approximate and intended as a guide for practice, not a phonetic chart.
